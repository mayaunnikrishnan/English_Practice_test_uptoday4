Answer Keys and Explanations

1. Answer: B) I don't have a car.

Explanation: For present possession (Have/Has), negative sentences use 'Don't have' (for I, You, We, They) and 'Doesn't have' (for He, She, It).

2. Answer: B) Do you have a car?

Explanation: Yes/No questions for present possession start with 'Do' or 'Does' followed by the subject and 'have'.

3. Answer: A) What

Explanation: WH-questions for possession follow the structure: WH + do/does + subject + have?

4. Answer: C) I didn't have money then.

Explanation: Past possession negatives use 'Didn't have' regardless of the subject pronoun.

5. Answer: B) Did you have money then?

Explanation: Past Yes/No questions use 'Did ... have?'.

6. Answer: A) I will not come tomorrow.

Explanation: Future negative uses 'Will not' (or 'Won't') + base verb.

7. Answer: A) When will you come?

Explanation: WH future questions follow: WH + will + subject + verb?

8. Answer: A) He won't be there.

Explanation: Future state negative uses 'Won't be' (Will not be).

9. Answer: B) Where will he be tomorrow?

Explanation: Future state WH questions follow: WH + will + subject + be?

10. Answer: A) He is going to buy a car.

Explanation: Immediate future uses Am/Is/Are + going to + verb.

11. Answer: A) You aren't going to buy a car.

Explanation: Immediate future negative uses Am/Is/Are not + going to.

12. Answer: A) What are you going to buy?

Explanation: Immediate future WH questions follow: WH + is/are/am + subject + going to + verb?

13. Answer: A) I have to do this / I must do this.

Explanation: Obligations/rules are expressed using Have to / Should / Must.

14. Answer: A) You don't have to do this / You shouldn't do this.

Explanation: Negative obligations use Don't have to / Shouldn't.

15. Answer: A) What should I do?

Explanation: WH obligation questions use WH + should / have to.

16. Answer: B) He has a house.

Explanation: Third-person singular pronouns (He, She, It) use 'has' for present possession.

17. Answer: C) He doesn't have a car.

Explanation: Third-person singular negative possession uses 'doesn't have'.

18. Answer: A) Didn't he have money then?

Explanation: Past negative questions follow: Didn't + subject + have?

19. Answer: A) Will he come tomorrow?

Explanation: Future Yes/No questions start with 'Will'.

20. Answer: B) Will he be there?

Explanation: Future state Yes/No questions follow: Will + subject + be?

21. Answer: A) Aren't you going to buy a car?

Explanation: Immediate future negative questions use Aren't / Isn't.

22. Answer: A) Do I have to do this? / Should I do this?

Explanation: Obligation questions use Do you have to? / Should I?

23. Answer: B) Shouldn't I do this? / Don't you have to do this?

Explanation: Negative obligation questions use Don't you have to? / Shouldn't I?

24. Answer: A) Won't you come tomorrow?

Explanation: Future negative questions use 'Won't ...?'.

25. Answer: A) He had a job then.

Explanation: Past possession uses 'had' for all subjects.
